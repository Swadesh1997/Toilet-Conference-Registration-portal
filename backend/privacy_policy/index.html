Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to the Toilet Conference 2025 mobile application (the "App"), provided by WaterAid. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the App.

Information Collection

We collect personal information that you provide to us directly, including but not limited to:

  • Your name and email address when you sign up for event updates.
  • Your feedback and event interest details.
  • Camera permissions for QR code scanning.

How We Use Your Information

Your information will be used for the following purposes:

  • To send you updates and notifications regarding Toilet Conference 2025 events.
  • To allow you to submit feedback and express interest in events.
  • To facilitate QR code scanning for event registrations and related purposes.

Camera Permissions

The App requires camera permissions to scan QR codes during the event. This permission will only be used for scanning QR codes and will not be used for any other purpose without your consent.

Data Protection

We take appropriate measures to safeguard your personal data and ensure it is protected against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please note that no data transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Third-Party Services

We may share your information with trusted third-party services for the purpose of sending notifications, processing feedback, or facilitating event registrations. These third parties are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it solely for the services provided to us.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the contact details provided below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and we will notify you of significant changes through the App.
